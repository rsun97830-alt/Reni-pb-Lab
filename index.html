Lesson 01 — Interest Rates

What is an interest rate?

An interest rate is the cost of borrowing money or the return earned from lending money.

利率是借入资金的成本，也可以是借出资金所获得的回报。

Example: A $10,000 deposit earning 3% simple annual interest generates $300 in one year, before fees and taxes.
